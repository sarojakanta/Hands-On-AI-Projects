Bright Career Institute — Admission Policy
==========================================

Overview
--------
Bright Career Institute admits students into its competitive-exam coaching
batches throughout the year, subject to seat availability. All information
below is fictional and for demonstration purposes only.

How to apply
------------
1. Submit an enquiry through our website chat, by phone, or in person.
2. A counsellor explains the batch, schedule, fee and syllabus coverage.
3. Complete the registration form and pay the registration fee to reserve a
   seat.
4. Receive your batch start date, student ID and study plan.

Documents required
-------------------
- One government-issued photo ID.
- One passport-size photograph.
- Proof of qualification relevant to the target exam (marksheet/certificate).

Fees and payment
----------------
- The course fee for each programme is listed on the course page and
  confirmed by a counsellor at enquiry time.
- A registration fee of INR 1,500 is part of (not additional to) the total
  course fee and reserves your seat.
- Fees may be paid in full or in two instalments; the second instalment is
  due within 30 days of the batch start.
- Accepted payment methods: UPI, bank transfer, debit/credit card, and cash.

Refund policy
-------------
- A full refund of the course fee (minus the registration fee) is available if
  cancellation is requested at least 5 days before the batch start date.
- A 40% refund is available if cancellation is requested within the first week
  of the batch.
- No refund is available after the first week of the batch.

Scholarships and discounts
--------------------------
- Merit scholarship of up to 20% for students with strong academic records or
  prior exam scores (counsellor assessment required).
- Early-bird discount of 10% for registering at least 15 days before the batch
  start date.

Test series and materials
--------------------------
Every coaching programme includes a mock-test series, previous-year question
analysis, and printed plus digital study material.

Contact
-------
For anything not covered here, ask to speak with a counsellor.
