Bright Career Institute — Frequently Asked Questions
====================================================

Q: Where is the institute located?
A: We are at 1st Floor, Vidya Towers, Station Road, Pune 411001, close to the
   railway station and several bus stops.

Q: What are your working hours?
A: The office is open Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM. We are closed on
   Sundays except for scheduled weekend batches.

Q: Which exams do you coach for?
A: We coach for banking exams (IBPS, SBI), SSC (CGL, CHSL), railway exams
   (RRB NTPC, Group D), and offer a general aptitude training course.

Q: Do you provide a mock-test series?
A: Yes. Every coaching programme includes a full mock-test series with
   detailed solutions and performance analysis.

Q: Are classes online or in a classroom?
A: Banking and SSC are classroom (offline), Railway is hybrid, and Aptitude
   Training is online. Confirm the current mode with a counsellor.

Q: Do you help with exam form filling?
A: Yes, our counsellors guide enrolled students through official exam
   notifications and application form submission.

Q: Can I pay the fee in instalments?
A: Yes, fees can be paid in two instalments, with the second due within 30
   days of the batch start.

Q: Is there a scholarship?
A: Merit scholarships of up to 20% are available based on academic records or
   prior exam scores, assessed by a counsellor.

Q: Do you provide study material?
A: Yes, printed and digital material, practice sets, and previous-year papers
   are included in every programme.
