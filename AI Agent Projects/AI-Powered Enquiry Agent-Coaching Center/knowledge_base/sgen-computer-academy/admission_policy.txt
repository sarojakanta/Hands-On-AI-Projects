SGEN Computer Academy — Admission Policy
========================================

Overview
--------
SGEN Computer Academy welcomes applications throughout the year. Admissions
are processed on a first-come, first-served basis subject to seat
availability in each batch. All information below is for demonstration
purposes only.

How to apply
------------
1. Submit an admission enquiry through our website chat, phone, or in person.
2. A counsellor confirms course details, batch timing, and fee.
3. Complete the registration form and pay the registration fee to confirm
   your seat.
4. Receive a confirmation message with your batch start date and joining
   instructions.

Documents required
-------------------
- One government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, etc.).
- One passport-size photograph.
- Proof of highest qualification (marksheet or certificate), if available.
Students below 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian at registration.

Fees and payment
-----------------
- The course fee for each course is listed on the course page and confirmed
  by a counsellor at enquiry time.
- A registration fee of INR 2,000 is part of (not additional to) the total
  course fee and confirms your seat.
- Fees may be paid in full or in two instalments. The first instalment is due
  before the batch start date; the second is due within 30 days of starting.
- Accepted payment methods: UPI, bank transfer, debit/credit card, and cash
  at the front desk.

Refund policy
-------------
- A full refund of the course fee (minus the registration fee) is available if
  cancellation is requested at least 3 days before the batch start date.
- A 50% refund is available if cancellation is requested within the first week
  of the batch.
- No refund is available after the first week of the batch.

Discounts
---------
- Early-bird discount of 10% when you register at least 20 days before the
  batch start date.
- Group discount of 10% for three or more students enrolling together.
- Discounts cannot be combined.

Certificates
------------
A course completion certificate is awarded to students who attend at least
80% of classes and complete the final project or assessment.

Contact
-------
For anything not covered here, ask to speak with a counsellor and we will be
happy to help.
