# Delivery Policy

## Delivery Attempts
Couriers make up to **three delivery attempts**. If all attempts fail, the
package is returned to the nearest hub and held for 3 days before being sent
back to origin.

## Delivery Delays
Delays can occur due to weather, regional disruptions, or high-volume periods.
If a shipment is marked delayed, the expected delivery date is updated in the
tracking information. We do not guarantee a specific delivery date for delayed
shipments.

## Address Changes
Delivery address changes can only be requested **before the order is
dispatched**. Once a shipment is in transit, the address cannot be changed.
Address-change requests require identity verification and are handled by
support.

## Failed Delivery and Refusal
If a customer refuses delivery or the address is undeliverable, the order is
returned and refunded under the Return Policy once received back, minus any
non-refundable shipping fees.

## Safe Delivery
For high-value orders, an OTP or signature may be required at the doorstep to
complete delivery.
