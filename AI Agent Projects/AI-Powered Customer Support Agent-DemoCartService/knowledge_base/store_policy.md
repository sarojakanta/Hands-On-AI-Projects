# Store Policy

## Store Hours
Store hours vary by location. Most stores are open **Monday to Saturday from
10:00 to 21:00**, with reduced hours on Sundays. Specific hours for each store
are available through the store locator.

## In-Store Returns and Exchanges
Items bought online can be returned or exchanged at any physical store within
the standard return window, provided the original invoice is presented. Online
Final Sale items remain non-returnable in store.

## In-Store Pickup
Select items support reserve-and-collect. Reserved items are held at the chosen
store for **48 hours**. Identity and order reference are required at pickup.

## Store Services
Physical stores offer product demonstrations, warranty drop-off for eligible
items, and assistance with returns and exchanges. Stores do not process cash
refunds for online card payments; such refunds go back to the original payment
method.

## Holidays
Stores may operate on reduced hours or remain closed on national public
holidays. Check the store locator for holiday schedules.
