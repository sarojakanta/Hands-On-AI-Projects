# Return Policy

## Return Window
Most items can be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full
refund. Items returned after 30 days but within 45 days are eligible for store
credit only. Returns are not accepted after 45 days from delivery.

## Condition Requirements
Returned items must be unused, in their original packaging, and include all
tags, manuals, and accessories. Items showing signs of use or damage may be
refused or subject to a partial refund.

## Non-Returnable Items
The following cannot be returned:
- Perishable goods and food items.
- Personal care and hygiene products once opened.
- Digital downloads and gift cards.
- Items marked "Final Sale".

## Refund Method and Timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once we receive and inspect
the returned item, refunds are processed within **5 to 7 business days**. The
time for the amount to appear on your statement depends on your bank.

## How to Start a Return
Start a return from your order history or by contacting support with your order
reference. A prepaid return label is provided for eligible returns within India.

## Return Shipping Costs
Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For change-of-mind
returns, a flat return shipping fee of INR 99 is deducted from the refund.
