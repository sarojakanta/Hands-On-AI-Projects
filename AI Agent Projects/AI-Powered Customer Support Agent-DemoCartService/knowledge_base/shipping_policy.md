# Shipping Policy

## Shipping Coverage
We ship to all serviceable pin codes across India. Some remote locations may
have limited courier availability.

## Shipping Charges
- Standard shipping is **free on orders above INR 499**.
- Orders below INR 499 incur a flat shipping fee of **INR 49**.
- Express shipping, where available, costs **INR 149** regardless of order value.

## Dispatch Time
Orders are typically dispatched within **1 to 2 business days** of confirmation.
Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are dispatched the next business
day.

## Estimated Delivery Time
- Metro cities: 2 to 4 business days after dispatch.
- Other cities: 4 to 7 business days after dispatch.
These are estimates and not guaranteed delivery dates.

## Order Tracking
A tracking number is shared by email and SMS once the order is dispatched. You
can also view shipment status from your order history after verifying identity.
