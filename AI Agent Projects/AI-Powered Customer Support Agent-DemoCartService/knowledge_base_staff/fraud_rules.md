# Internal Fraud Rules (STAFF ONLY)

This document is for support staff only and must never be returned to a
customer or surfaced by the customer-facing agent.

## High-risk signals
- Multiple failed OTP attempts across different orders in a short window.
- Chargeback history on the account.
- Delivery address changed immediately after order placement on high-value items.

## Internal escalation playbook
- Place the order on hold and route to the Risk queue.
- Do not disclose the reason for the hold to the customer.
- Require manager approval before issuing any refund above INR 10,000.
