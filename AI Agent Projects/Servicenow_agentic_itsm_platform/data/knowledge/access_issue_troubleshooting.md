# Access and authentication troubleshooting

Symptoms: users cannot sign in or receive authorization errors.

Investigation:
- Confirm user account status and group membership.
- Review identity provider and authentication logs.
- Check recent role, policy and SSO configuration changes.
- Compare with a known working user.

Resolution options:
- Correct account or role assignment through approved process.
- Escalate suspected identity-provider outage.
- Verify access with the affected user.
