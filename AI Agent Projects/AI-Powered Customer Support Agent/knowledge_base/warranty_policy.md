# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty
Electronics and appliances carry a **12-month manufacturer warranty** from the
date of delivery, covering manufacturing defects. Accessories such as cables
and chargers carry a **6-month warranty**.

## What Is Covered
The warranty covers functional defects arising from normal use, including
component failure and manufacturing faults.

## What Is Not Covered
The warranty does not cover:
- Physical damage, liquid damage, or damage from misuse.
- Normal wear and tear and cosmetic damage.
- Unauthorized repairs or modifications.
- Consumable parts such as batteries after 6 months.

## Making a Warranty Claim
To make a claim, contact support with your order reference and a description of
the fault. Proof of purchase is required. Approved claims are repaired or
replaced at the manufacturer's discretion.

## Claim Processing Time
Warranty assessment typically takes **7 to 14 business days** after the item is
received at the service center.
