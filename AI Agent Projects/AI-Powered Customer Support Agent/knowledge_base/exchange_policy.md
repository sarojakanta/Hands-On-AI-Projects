# Exchange Policy

## Exchange Window
Items may be exchanged within **30 days** of delivery for a different size,
color, or variant of the same product, subject to availability.

## Eligibility
Exchanged items must be unused and in original packaging with all tags. One
exchange is allowed per item. If the replacement variant has a different price,
the difference is either charged or refunded accordingly.

## Out-of-Stock Replacements
If the requested replacement variant is unavailable, you may choose a refund
under the Return Policy or wait for restock. We do not guarantee restock dates.

## Exchange Shipping
For defective or incorrect items, exchange shipping is free both ways. For
change-of-mind exchanges, standard outbound shipping charges apply to the
replacement item.

## How to Request an Exchange
Request an exchange from your order history or by contacting support with your
order reference and the desired replacement variant.
