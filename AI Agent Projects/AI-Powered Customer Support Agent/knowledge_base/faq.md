# Frequently Asked Questions

## How do I track my order?
Once your order is dispatched, a tracking number is sent by email and SMS. You
can also check shipment status in your order history after verifying your
identity with a one-time code.

## Why is my order still processing?
Orders are usually dispatched within 1 to 2 business days of confirmation.
During sales or holidays, processing can take slightly longer.

## Can I change my delivery address after ordering?
Address changes are only possible before the order is dispatched and require
identity verification. Once in transit, the address cannot be changed.

## How long do refunds take?
After we receive and inspect a returned item, refunds are processed within 5 to
7 business days to the original payment method.

## Do you offer cash on delivery?
Cash on delivery is available on eligible orders below a value threshold in
serviceable areas. Availability is shown at checkout.

## How do I contact a human agent?
You can ask to speak with a human at any time, and we will connect you with a
support specialist. Sensitive issues are always escalated to a person.

## Is my personal information safe?
We only reveal order and account details after successful identity
verification, and we never share your information between accounts.
